# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a straightforward place to do good engineering.
Everyone taking part — contributors, reviewers, maintainers, people opening
issues — is expected to act professionally and in good faith.

## Expected behaviour

- Review the work, not the person. Criticise a design, a claim or a patch; do
  not characterise the author.
- Be direct and specific. "This breaks X because Y" is useful; "this is wrong"
  is not.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a contribution, and say why once.
- Respect the project's boundaries: no real tenant data in issues or pull
  requests (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)), and no response actions executed
  against a production tenant to make a point.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory or demeaning remarks, sustained personal hostility,
publishing others' private information, and deliberately derailing discussions.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages and code review — and whenever someone is representing the
project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on
this repository, or to the repository owners directly. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or delete content, or block an account,
and will say what they did and why.
